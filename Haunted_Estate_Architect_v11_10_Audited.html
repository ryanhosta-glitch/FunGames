4. Openings & Features

Easy placement: ① Select a room on the floor plan. ② Choose a door/window shape. ③ Click Add. ④ A big wall chooser appears over the floor plan. Click TOP, RIGHT, BOTTOM, or LEFT. You can also click the highlighted wall directly.
Vault hatch: select a room above another level. A secret door uses the ordinary wall-opening placement tool; it must connect to a neighboring room.
Tip: You do not have to click a tiny line. Once placement mode starts, click anywhere along one of the four bright wall targets. The opening snaps into place. Doors and windows are decorative and do not subtract from room volume.
Site & Architectural Features
Placement: Click a haunted detail, then click where you want it on the floor plan. Dead trees, fences, gravestones, and porch columns can go anywhere. Chimneys, dormers, shutters, and turrets should be placed on or beside the house. Drag a placed detail to move it. Decorations do not change room volume.
Placed Items
